Bebas Neue, Inter, and Source Serif 4 are sourced from
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/cc5661d43cd4f9aad5567033a8e15ca28dc35c86/ofl
and repackaged as WOFF2 without subsetting. Inter and Source Serif 4
keep their variable weights. Their optical size is fixed at the font
default so preview scaling cannot change the glyph widths.

The IBM Plex Mono WOFF2 files are published by IBM at
https://github.com/IBM/plex/tree/2e90fe366fdc27c12682db13e5aa20ded3d90908/packages/plex-mono/fonts/complete/woff2

See each family's OFL.txt for copyright and license information.
